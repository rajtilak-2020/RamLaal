                    🤖 RAMLAAL - SERVER BOT GUIDE 🤖
        (Share this with your server members & Discord friends!)
================================================================================

Meet RamLaal! He is our 24/7 server companion. He walks around, talks back,
roasts players, and helps us skip the night!

Here is everything you can do with him in the game:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🛌 1. SLEEP & SKIP NIGHT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Type: !sleep  (or "soja", "soja ramlaal")
  ➔ RamLaal will walk to his bed and sleep to skip the night!

• Type: !wake   (or "uth ja", "uth")
  ➔ Wakes RamLaal up from his bed in the morning.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🕺 2. FUN MOVES & ANIMATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Type these in public chat and watch him perform:

• !twerk  (or "mujra")    ➔ RamLaal will look at you and twerk! 💃
• !jump   (or "dance")    ➔ RamLaal jumps and shows his dance moves! 🕺
• !fight  (or "punch")    ➔ RamLaal starts throwing rapid punches in the air! 🥊
• !spin   (or "beyblade") ➔ RamLaal spins 360 degrees like a Beyblade! 🌪️


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🧠 3. AI MINI-GAMES & COMMANDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• !iq                  ➔ Scans your brain and calculates your Minecraft IQ!
• !roast <player>      ➔ Generates a savage roast for you or your friend!
• !shayari             ➔ Recites funny Desi Minecraft poetry!
• !fortune             ➔ Predicts your luck (Creepers, Diamonds, or Death)!
• !calc <math>         ➔ Solves math problems (e.g. !calc 50*2+10)
• !toss (or "coin")    ➔ Flips a coin for Heads or Tails!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
💬 4. TALK TO RAMLAAL IN CHAT (He talks back!)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RamLaal listens to player messages, looks at you, and replies:

• Say "hi" / "hello" / "kaisa hai"
  ➔ He greets you like a tired 24/7 worker.

• Say "ramlaal" or "ramlal"
  ➔ He turns his head to you and asks what you need.

• Call him a "noob" / "bot" / roast him
  ➔ Be careful! He will roast your dirt house and your PvP skills.

• Ask for "diamond" / "khana" / "iron"
  ➔ He complains that he is broke himself and tells you to go mining.

• Praise him ("op", "pro", "king", "goat")
  ➔ He jumps with pride and flexes his supreme AI status.

• Ask "who made you" / "tera baap kon hai"
  ➔ He tells you who created him.

• Ask about his "girlfriend" / "crush"
  ➔ He cries about Alex leaving him for Steve.

• Say "bye" / "tata" / "ja raha hu"
  ➔ He gives a savage goodbye.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⚠️ 5. SURPRISE REACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Stand too close to him (< 2 blocks):
  ➔ He will snap his neck, stare right into your eyes, and tell you to back off!

• Punch or Hit him:
  ➔ He swings his arm back, defends himself, and screams at you!

• If you die near him:
  ➔ He will roast your death in public chat for the whole server to see!

• When rain starts:
  ➔ He complains about the weather and asks for chai-pakode!
