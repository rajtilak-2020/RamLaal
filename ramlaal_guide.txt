================================================================================
                    🤖 RAMLAAL - ADORABLE COMPANION GUIDE 🤖
        (Share this with your server members & Discord friends!)
================================================================================

Meet RamLaal! He is our adorable, living base companion. He wanders around the
base, waves, nods, cuddles in bed, and loves hanging out with everyone!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🏠 1. ADORABLE "WELCOME HOME" REACTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• When you return to the base from mining or exploring:
  ➔ RamLaal spots you, does the universal Minecraft crouch-spam greeting,
     waves his arm excitedly, and celebrates your return:
     "Yay! [Player] wapas aa gaye! Welcome home! 🥰✨"
• When you walk close to him:
  ➔ He stops, crouches to say hello, and waves like a real human player!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🥰 2. WHOLESOME & CUTE COMMANDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Type these in public chat to interact with him:

• !hug (or "gale mil", "pyaar")
  ➔ RamLaal crouches, waves his arms, and gives you a warm virtual hug! 🤗❤️

• !pat (or "headpat", "pucho")
  ➔ RamLaal nods his head happily and purrs from the headpat! 🥰🐾

• !flower (or "phool", "gift")
  ➔ RamLaal delivers a sweet virtual flower to you! 🌸🌼

• !sing (or "gaana", "song")
  ➔ RamLaal sings a cute funny Minecraft song for the server! 🎶🎤

• !compliment (or "tareef")
  ➔ RamLaal tells you why you are the best player on the server! 💖✨


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🕺 3. FUN MOVES & DANCES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• !twerk (or "mujra")    ➔ RamLaal twerks up and down! 💃
• !jump (or "dance")     ➔ RamLaal hops with joy! 🕺
• !spin (or "beyblade")  ➔ RamLaal spins 360 degrees! 🌪️
• !fight (or "punch")    ➔ RamLaal playfully punches the air! 🥊


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🛌 4. COZY BEDTIME & MORNING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Bedtime: At night, he hops into bed and wishes everyone sweet dreams with his teddy bear! 🧸🛌
• Morning: Wakes up shouting "Good morning sunshine! ☀️🌸"
• Commands:
  - !sleep (or "soja") ➔ Forces him to bed immediately.
  - !wake (or "uth ja") ➔ Wakes him up.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🧠 5. AI MINI-GAMES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• !iq                  ➔ Scans your brain and gives your Minecraft IQ!
• !roast <player>      ➔ Generates a funny roast for a friend!
• !shayari             ➔ Recites hilarious Desi Minecraft poetry!
• !fortune             ➔ Predicts your luck (Creepers, Diamonds, or Death)!
• !calc <math>         ➔ Solves math problems (e.g. !calc 50*2+10)
• !toss (or "coin")    ➔ Flips a coin for Heads or Tails!
