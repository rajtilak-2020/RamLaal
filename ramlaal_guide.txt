                🤖 RAMLAAL - ULTRA-REALISTIC COMPANION GUIDE 🤖
        (Share this with your server members & Discord friends!)
================================================================================

RamLaal is an ultra-realistic companion bot! He mirrors player body language,
fidgets with hotbar items, inspects builds, fights hostile mobs fearlessly,
sprints to bed ONLY when players sleep, and deposits his inventory to a chest
every morning!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🧍‍♂️ 1. BODY-LANGUAGE MIRRORING (He Copies Your Moves!)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Crouch Mirroring:
  ➔ Walk up to RamLaal and CROUCH. He will immediately crouch back in rhythm
     like real Minecraft players do to greet each other!
• Jump Mirroring:
  ➔ Jump in front of him, and he will do a happy hop with you!
• Arm Swing:
  ➔ Swing your arm at him, and he will do a friendly wave back!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🧱 2. CURIOUS BUILDING INSPECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Whenever you or a friend place a block near RamLaal (within 5 blocks):
  ➔ He hears the block place, turns his head to look directly at the new block,
     nods in approval, and compliments your architecture!
     "Arre wah! Naya block lagaya, badhiya build lag raha hai 🧱✨"


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🎮 3. REAL HUMAN HABITS & PHYSICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Organic Mouse Camera:
  Smooth S-curve head turns with subtle micro-movements simulating a human hand
  resting on a mouse (no jerky robot snaps).
• Hotbar Fidgeting:
  Every 20-30 seconds, he cycles items in his hand (tools, bread, flowers, etc.)
  just like restless players cycling their 1-5 hotbar keys.
• Realistic Typing Delays:
  Before he speaks in chat, he looks at you and pauses for 1-2 seconds,
  simulating real keyboard typing before sending his message!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
👀 4. DISTANT VISION & EYE-CONTACT RECOGNITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Watching from afar (6 to 22 blocks):
  ➔ While patrolling, RamLaal notices players walking in the distance and
     smoothly turns his head to watch them like a curious friend.
• Eye-Contact Recognition:
  ➔ When you look directly at him with your crosshair from afar, he senses
     your gaze, waves his arm, and nods back at you! (100% silent & realistic)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🛌 5. PLAYER-TRIGGERED BED SPRINT (Sleeps ONLY when another player sleeps!)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Awake All Night By Default:
  ➔ RamLaal will NEVER go to sleep automatically just because it is night.
     He stays awake guarding your base and enjoying the night!
• Instant Sprint When ANY Player Sleeps:
  ➔ The exact moment AT LEAST ONE player on the server gets into a bed,
     RamLaal instantly detects it!
  ➔ He sprints at top speed toward his nearby bed (up to 18 blocks away),
     auto-jumping over obstacles, and hops in immediately to skip the night!
• Manual Sleep Command:
  ➔ You can also command him manually with "!sleep" or "soja" in chat.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📦 6. MORNING CHEST INVENTORY CLEANUP (Excludes Sword & Armor)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Automatic Morning Deposit:
  ➔ Every morning when he wakes up (or at dawn), RamLaal walks over to the
     chest or barrel placed near his bed/base.
  ➔ He opens the chest and automatically deposits all mob drops, cobblestone,
     dirt, and gathered items into the chest!
• Equipment Protection (Keeps his gear!):
  ➔ He will NEVER put his weapons or gear into the chest! All swords, helmets,
     chestplates, leggings, boots, and shields remain safely in his possession.
• On-Demand Deposit Command:
  ➔ Type "!deposit", "!chest", or "!clearinv" in chat anytime to make him
     store his inventory items into the nearby chest right away!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
⚔️ 7. AGGRESSIVE BASE DEFENSE & AUTO-RETURN (CREATIVE BOSS MODE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Fearless Monster Hunter:
  ➔ RamLaal scans 16 blocks around him for ANY hostile mobs (Zombies, Creepers,
     Skeletons, Spiders, Phantoms, Endermen, Witches, etc.).
  ➔ Since he is in Creative Mode, he has ZERO fear! He sprint-charges directly
     at monsters at maximum speed!
• Jump-Critical Combos:
  ➔ When within reach (<= 3.8 blocks), he performs jump-critical strikes,
     swinging his sword with rapid combos to slice monsters down quickly.
• Pursuit Beyond Block Limit:
  ➔ He is allowed to chase mobs up to 32 blocks away from his base anchor to
     ensure all threats near your base are completely eliminated!
• Automatic Return to Base Spawn:
  ➔ The moment all nearby monsters are dead, RamLaal automatically turns around,
     sprints straight back to his original base/spawn anchor, and resumes his
     peaceful patrol within his 7-block home zone!


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🔇 8. MUTE & UNMUTE CHAT CONTROLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Want total silence in the chat, or ready to chat again? Use these:

• !mute (or "chup", "shutup", "!silent")
  ➔ Completely MUTES RamLaal from saying anything in chat!
     He stays 100% quiet while still performing dances, hugs, and base guarding.

• !unmute (or "bol", "!speak", "speak", "!talk")
  ➔ UNMUTES RamLaal! He can now speak, roast, and reply to you in chat again.

• !status
  ➔ Checks whether RamLaal is currently Muted or Speaking, base status, and HP.

• !help (or !commands)
  ➔ Lists available commands right in your Minecraft chat.
