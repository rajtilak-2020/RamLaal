# 🤖 RAMLAAL - SERVER BOT GUIDE 🤖
> *Meet RamLaal! Our 24/7 server companion who keeps the server online, talks back, copies your moves, guards the base, and stores loot in your chest!*

────────────────────────────────────────────

### 🛌 1. SLEEP & SKIP NIGHT (Smart Player-Triggered Sprint)
* **Awake All Night by Default:** RamLaal will NEVER go to sleep automatically just because it is night. He stays awake patrolling and guarding the base!
* **Player Sleep Trigger:** The exact moment **AT LEAST ONE player** on the server gets into a bed, RamLaal detects it instantly and **sprints at full speed** to his bed to hop in and skip the night!
* `!sleep` (or `soja`) ➔ Manually tells RamLaal to sprint to his bed and sleep.
* `!wake` (or `uth ja`) ➔ Wakes RamLaal up from his bed.

────────────────────────────────────────────

### 📦 2. MORNING CHEST INVENTORY CLEANUP (Keeps Sword & Armor!)
* **Morning Auto-Deposit:** Every morning (upon waking up or dawn), RamLaal walks to the chest or barrel placed near his bed.
* **Stores Extra Loot:** He deposits all collected items, mob drops, dirt, cobblestone, etc. into the chest!
* **Gear Protection:** He **never** stores your weapons or armor! All swords, helmets, chestplates, leggings, boots, and shields stay safely on him!
* `!deposit` (or `!chest`, `!clearinv`) ➔ Manually command him to dump extra inventory items into the nearby chest anytime!

────────────────────────────────────────────

### 🔇 3. MUTE & UNMUTE CHAT CONTROLS
* `!mute` (or `chup`, `shutup`, `!silent`) ➔ **Completely MUTES RamLaal!**
  * He will stay 100% silent in chat while still dancing, doing twerks, hugs, and guarding base!
* `!unmute` (or `bol`, `!speak`, `speak`) ➔ **UNMUTES RamLaal!**
  * Makes him speak, chat, and answer questions again!
* `!status` ➔ Shows if RamLaal is Muted or Speaking, base status, sleeping players count, and HP.
* `!help` ➔ Shows quick command list in Minecraft chat.

────────────────────────────────────────────

### 🧍‍♂️ 4. BODY-LANGUAGE MIRRORING (He Copies You!)
* **Crouch:** Walk up to RamLaal and **CROUCH** ➔ He immediately crouches back like a real player!
* **Jump:** Hop in front of him ➔ He does a happy hop with you!
* **Arm Swing:** Swing your arm at him ➔ He gives you a friendly arm wave back!

────────────────────────────────────────────

### 👀 5. DISTANT VISION & EYE-CONTACT RECOGNITION
* **Watching from Far:** When you are working 10–20 blocks away, RamLaal smoothly turns his head to watch you like a curious friend.
* **Eye-Contact Recognition:** If you aim your crosshair directly at him from far away, he detects your gaze, waves, and nods back at you!

────────────────────────────────────────────

### 🧱 6. CURIOUS BUILDING INSPECTION
* Whenever you place blocks near him (within 5 blocks):
  * He hears the block place, turns his head toward your new block, and nods in approval!

────────────────────────────────────────────

### ⚔️ 7. AGGRESSIVE BASE DEFENSE & AUTO-RETURN (CREATIVE BOSS MODE)
* **Fearless Monster Hunter:**
  * RamLaal scans 16 blocks around him for ANY hostile mobs (Zombies, Creepers, Skeletons, Spiders, etc.).
  * In Creative Mode, he has ZERO fear! He sprint-charges directly at monsters at full speed!
* **Jump-Critical Combos:**
  * Slices mobs with jump-critical attacks and rapid sword combos.
* **Pursuit Beyond Block Limit:**
  * Chases monsters up to 32 blocks away from base anchor to exterminate all threats.
* **Automatic Return to Base:**
  * The instant all monsters are defeated, RamLaal sprints right back to his original spawn/base location and resumes normal patrol!

────────────────────────────────────────────

* `!pat` (or `headpat`, `pucho`) ➔ Nods happily and purrs from the headpat! 🥰🐾
* `!flower` (or `phool`) ➔ Delivers a sweet virtual flower to you! 🌸🌼
* `!sing` (or `gaana`) ➔ Sings a cute funny Minecraft song! 🎶🎤
* `!compliment` (or `tareef`) ➔ Tells you why you are the best player! 💖✨
* `!setbase` (or `yahan raho`) ➔ Locks his base home anchor right to your current spot! 📍
* `!deposit` (or `!chest`) ➔ Cleans extra items into the nearby chest! 📦

────────────────────────────────────────────

### 💬 11. TALK TO HIM NATURALLY IN CHAT
When unmuted, RamLaal understands natural talk and replies directly while looking at you:
* Say `hi` / `hello` / `kaisa hai` ➔ Friendly greetings.
* Say `ramlaal` ➔ Asks what you need.
* Call him a `noob` or `bot` ➔ He roasts your dirt house and your PvP skills!
* Ask for `diamond` or `khana` ➔ He complains about being broke himself.
* Praise him (`op`, `pro`, `king`) ➔ He jumps with pride.
* Say `bye` / `tata` ➔ Gives a funny farewell.
