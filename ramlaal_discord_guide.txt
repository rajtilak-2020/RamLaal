# 🤖 RAMLAAL - SERVER BOT GUIDE 🤖
> *Meet RamLaal! Our 24/7 server companion who keeps the server online, talks back, copies your moves, and guards the base!*

────────────────────────────────────────────

### 🛌 1. SLEEP & SKIP NIGHT (Silent Instant Bed Sprint)
* `!sleep` (or `soja`) ➔ RamLaal sprints at full speed to his bed and hops in to skip the night!
* `!wake` (or `uth ja`) ➔ Wakes RamLaal up from his bed.
> **Note:** The exact millisecond night arrives, he automatically sprints to bed on his own silently (zero chat spam, peaceful night skip)!

────────────────────────────────────────────

### 🔇 2. MUTE & UNMUTE CHAT CONTROLS (NEW!)
* `!mute` (or `chup`, `shutup`, `!silent`) ➔ **Completely MUTES RamLaal!**
  * He will stay 100% silent in chat while still dancing, doing twerks, hugs, and guarding base!
* `!unmute` (or `bol`, `!speak`, `speak`) ➔ **UNMUTES RamLaal!**
  * Makes him speak, chat, and answer questions again!
* `!status` ➔ Shows if RamLaal is currently Muted or Speaking, base status, and HP.
* `!help` ➔ Shows quick command list in Minecraft chat.

────────────────────────────────────────────

### 🧍‍♂️ 3. BODY-LANGUAGE MIRRORING (He Copies You!)
* **Crouch:** Walk up to RamLaal and **CROUCH** ➔ He immediately crouches back like a real player!
* **Jump:** Hop in front of him ➔ He does a happy hop with you!
* **Arm Swing:** Swing your arm at him ➔ He gives you a friendly arm wave back!

────────────────────────────────────────────

### 👀 4. DISTANT VISION & EYE-CONTACT RECOGNITION
* **Watching from Far:** When you are working 10–20 blocks away, RamLaal smoothly turns his head to watch you like a curious friend.
* **Eye-Contact Recognition:** If you aim your crosshair directly at him from far away, he detects your gaze, waves, and nods back at you!

────────────────────────────────────────────

### 🧱 5. CURIOUS BUILDING INSPECTION
* Whenever you place blocks near him (within 5 blocks):
  * He hears the block place, turns his head toward your new block, and nods in approval!

────────────────────────────────────────────

### ⚔️ 6. AGGRESSIVE BASE DEFENSE & AUTO-RETURN (CREATIVE BOSS MODE)
* **Fearless Monster Hunter:**
  * RamLaal scans 16 blocks around him for ANY hostile mobs (Zombies, Creepers, Skeletons, Spiders, etc.).
  * In Creative Mode, he has ZERO fear! He sprint-charges directly at monsters at full speed!
* **Jump-Critical Combos:**
  * Slices mobs with jump-critical attacks and rapid sword combos.
* **Pursuit Beyond Block Limit:**
  * Chases monsters up to 32 blocks away from base anchor to exterminate all threats.
* **Automatic Return to Base:**
  * The instant all monsters are defeated, RamLaal sprints right back to his original spawn/base location and resumes normal patrol!

────────────────────────────────────────────

* `!flower` (or `phool`) ➔ Delivers a sweet virtual flower to you! 🌸🌼
* `!sing` (or `gaana`) ➔ Sings a cute funny Minecraft song! 🎶🎤
* `!compliment` (or `tareef`) ➔ Tells you why you are the best player! 💖✨

* Say `hi` / `hello` / `kaisa hai` ➔ Friendly greetings.
* Say `ramlaal` ➔ Asks what you need.
* Call him a `noob` or `bot` ➔ He roasts your dirt house and your PvP skills!
